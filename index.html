Travel Smart

Essential Travel Tips

Everything you need to know before your African adventure.

💉

Health & Vaccinations

Prepare your health well in advance. Required and recommended vaccinations for East Africa:

  • Yellow Fever (required for Tanzania, Uganda, Kenya entry from certain countries)
  • Malaria prophylaxis (consult your doctor 4–6 weeks before)
  • Hepatitis A & B, Typhoid, Rabies (recommended)
  • COVID-19 and routine vaccinations up to date
  • Bring sufficient prescription medications
🛂

Visas & Entry

Visa requirements vary by destination and nationality:

  • Tanzania: eVisa available online (eTanzania portal)
  • Kenya: eTA (Electronic Travel Authorisation) required
  • Uganda: e-Visa at evisa.go.ug
  • Rwanda: Visa on arrival or e-Visa; 30+ African nations visa-free
  • East Africa Tourist Visa: covers Kenya, Uganda, Rwanda (single $100 fee)
  • Valid passport: minimum 6 months beyond travel dates
🗓️

Best Time to Visit

Seasonal guide for East Africa:

  • Jan–Mar: Dry, excellent for Kilimanjaro; calving season on Serengeti
  • Jun–Oct: Best overall — dry season, Great Migration river crossings
  • Jul–Sep: Peak for Maasai Mara wildebeest crossings
  • Nov–Apr: Bird watching season, green landscapes, fewer tourists
  • Zanzibar: Year-round but Jun–Oct is driest for beaches
🎒

What to Pack

Safari packing essentials — light layers are key:

  • Neutral-coloured clothing (khaki, beige, olive) — avoid white/blue
  • Lightweight layers: mornings/evenings on safari can be cold
  • Wide-brim hat, UV-protective sunglasses, SPF 50+ sunscreen
  • Quality binoculars (8×42 or 10×42 recommended)
  • Camera with zoom lens; extra batteries and memory cards
  • Dust-proof dry bag or camera cover
  • Sturdy walking shoes + flip flops for beach
💰

Money & Budget Tips

Managing finances in East Africa:

  • USD is widely accepted in Tanzania, Kenya, Uganda; carry small bills
  • Notify your bank before travel; Visa/Mastercard widely accepted in cities
  • ATMs available in all major towns; M-Pesa (mobile money) dominant in Kenya
  • Budget safari: $150–$250/day | Mid-range: $350–$600/day | Luxury: $700+/day
  • Tipping: $10–20/day per guide; $5–10/day per porter on climbs
  • Park fees are NOT negotiable and must be budgeted separately
📡

Connectivity & Safety

Staying connected and safe on safari:

  • SIM cards: buy local (Vodacom/Airtel Tanzania, Safaricom Kenya)
  • Data is affordable — 10GB for ~$5 in Tanzania
  • WiFi available at lodges; remote camps may lack connectivity
  • Travel insurance with emergency evacuation is strongly recommended
  • East Africa is generally very safe for tourists in tourist areas
  • Always follow guide instructions in the bush — wildlife safety first
